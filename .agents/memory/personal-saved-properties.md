---
name: Personal saved properties
description: Role-independent access and terminology for a user's own property favorites.
---

Every signed-in account role can save another lister's property and access its own Saved Properties list on web, Android, and iOS.

**Why:** Owners and hosts can also browse as customers. "Property Likes" means people who saved the user's owned listings; it is not a substitute for the user's own favorites.

**How to apply:** Keep personal Saved navigation visible across authenticated roles, preserve a distinct owner-facing Property Likes view, and scope each personal favorites list to the authenticated user.
